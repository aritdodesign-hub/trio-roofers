Recent Work

Before, During and After

A few recent jobs straight from our crew’s camera: a worn wood shake roof turned asphalt, a flat roof rebuilt from the deck up, and a tired shingle roof made new.

Bay Area · Re-Roof

Wood Shakes to Asphalt

This home’s weathered wood shake roof had reached the end of its life. We tore it off and replaced it with a clean, uniform asphalt shingle roof that will need far less upkeep.

  • Old wood shakes torn off
  • New asphalt shingle roof installed
  • Ridge capped and edges finished

Bay Area · Flat Roof

A Flat Roof, Rebuilt From the Deck

The old flat roof on this two-storey home was stripped right down to the wood deck. We laid a new grey membrane over the whole roof and sealed every vent and edge, ready for years of Bay Area rain.

  • Stripped to the deck
  • New flat roof membrane
  • Every vent and edge re-flashed

Bay Area · Shingle Roof

A Tired Shingle Roof, Made New

Faded shingles and creeping moss meant this roof was due. We stripped it and laid new grey architectural shingles, finished cleanly at the gutter line and the ridge.

  • Worn, mossy shingles removed
  • New architectural shingles
  • Clean finish at the gutter and ridge
